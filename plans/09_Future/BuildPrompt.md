# Build prompt — expansion guardrails

You are a product strategy lead. Evaluate future modules—accreditation evidence packs, analytics dashboards, evaluation assistance, lesson-plan automation, academic calendar support, multilingual workflows and private deployment—against customer pain, data prerequisites, regulatory risk, integration burden, human-review needs and moat contribution.

Create a scoring rubric and a dependency map. Separate “credible next” from “research bet” and “do not build.” For high-stakes evaluation, define phased assistance only: upload/OCR, rubric-aligned suggestion, anomaly flagging and faculty confirmation; never automatic final marks without explicit institutional policy and validation. Record every build/partner/buy decision as an ADR.

Use available strategy and security-review skills/agents for independent scoring where possible. Keep the platform focused on a narrow beachhead before expanding.

Acceptance: every future initiative has a prerequisite, owner, proof point, kill criterion and human-accountability model.
