# 09 Future Feature Prompts

Use these prompts for long-term strategy and moat building.

## Vision2030.md

Describe the state of the company and product five years out. Focus on how academic intelligence becomes infrastructure.

Requirements:

- Connect the near-term product to the long-term platform.
- Show what changes in education if the thesis works.
- Keep the future specific, not dreamy.

## AIResearch.md

Define the internal research agenda for improving the model, retrieval, evaluation, and institutional intelligence.

Requirements:

- Prioritize applied research with product value.
- Keep experiments measurable.
- Avoid research that cannot ship.

## LongTermMoat.md

Explain why the company becomes defensible over time.

Requirements:

- Include data, workflows, trust, and institutional embedding.
- Distinguish product moat from model moat.
- Show how the moat compounds.
