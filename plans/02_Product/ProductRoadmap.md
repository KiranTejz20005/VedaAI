# Product roadmap
**Phase 1 / MVP (0–3 months):** Academic Knowledge Engine—ingestion, source review, curriculum graph, CO/PO/PSO mapping, versioned evidence and OBE-ready assessment planning.  
**Phase 2 (3–6 months):** governed AI pipeline—retrieval, one-item generation, validation, human review, export, answer keys and rubrics.  
**Phase 3 (6–12 months):** institutional dashboard—attainment analytics, internal marks workflows, accreditation evidence packs, ERP/LMS integration and role workflows.  
**V3 (12–24 months):** multi-programme graph, calibrated difficulty, evaluation support, multilingual authoring, secure enterprise deployment and benchmarking.

Advance only when adoption and quality gates are met.
