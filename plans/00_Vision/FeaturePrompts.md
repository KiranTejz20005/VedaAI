# 00 Vision Feature Prompts

Use these prompts when an agent needs to create or revise the vision layer.

## Vision.md

Write the company vision as an Academic Intelligence Operating System for higher education. Define the promise in one sentence, then expand it into the core beliefs, the transformation for institutions, and the long-term north star. Keep the wording precise, ambitious, and institution-facing.

Requirements:

- Explain why academic knowledge must become structured and machine-usable.
- State that the product improves curriculum planning, assessment, compliance, and research operations.
- Include the emotional outcome for faculty and leadership.
- Avoid generic AI startup language.

## Problem.md

Describe the real operational problem in universities and colleges. Focus on fragmented academic work, slow evidence collection, manual curriculum alignment, repeated reporting, and the gap between teaching intent and measurable outcomes.

Requirements:

- Show why this problem exists now.
- Separate symptoms from root causes.
- Mention OBE, CO-PO mapping, lesson planning, assessment evidence, and accreditation work.

## WhyNow.md

Explain why this product should be built now rather than later. Tie together policy pressure, digital adoption, increasing academic documentation load, and the rise of structured AI workflows.

Requirements:

- Explain the timing from the institution's perspective.
- Explain the timing from the technology perspective.
- Mention why a pure chatbot is no longer enough.

## StartupThesis.md

Turn the vision into a startup thesis. State the wedge, the buyer, the moat, the rollout path, and the reason this becomes durable.

Requirements:

- Define the first paid use case.
- Make the thesis specific to higher education.
- Include why the knowledge graph and evidence layer matter.
- End with the strategic bet in one paragraph.

## Alignment rule

If any of these prompts conflict with later product, AI, or startup documents, the later document should inherit the vision only after the vision is updated first.
