# Build prompt — pilot, sales and investor readiness

Act as a founder’s chief of staff. Create a design-partner pilot package for one engineering-college department: qualification criteria, mutual success plan, security/data questionnaire, implementation calendar, training plan, weekly review template, metric definitions, conversion proposal and case-study consent process. The promise is governed OBE/assessment evidence workflow improvement, not magic AI or unsupported workload-reduction percentages.

Create sales discovery questions that identify the economic buyer, compliance deadline, existing ERP/LMS, historical data rights and procurement constraints. Write an investor narrative that explains why VidyaAI is defensible: institutional graph, workflow integration, validation/evaluation, approvals and accumulated evidence—not owning an LLM. Keep claims evidence-backed and clearly label assumptions.

Use available research or writing-review agents only for critique, with no external outreach. Do not invent customer logos, benchmarks or regulatory certifications.

Acceptance: a pilot can be run in eight weeks with explicit data boundaries, a measurable baseline and an unambiguous paid-conversion decision.
