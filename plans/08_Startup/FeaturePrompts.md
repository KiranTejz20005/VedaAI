# 08 Startup Feature Prompts

Use these prompts for the commercial and operating side of VidyaAI.

## GTM.md

Describe the go-to-market motion for institutions. Include buyer targeting, messaging, channels, proof points, and rollout strategy.

Requirements:

- Start with the best beachhead segment.
- Tie messaging to workflow pain.
- Show how trust is earned.

## SalesStrategy.md

Define the sales process from first contact to pilot to expansion.

Requirements:

- Include stakeholders, objections, and proof needed.
- Explain how the sale maps to institutional cycles.
- Keep the motion realistic for a small team.

## PilotPlan.md

Design the pilot execution plan with timeline, success metrics, roles, and acceptance criteria.

Requirements:

- Make the pilot short and measurable.
- Include what the institution must provide.
- Define what success looks like in writing.

## PricingStrategy.md

Describe the pricing logic as a startup strategy, not just a price list.

Requirements:

- Connect price to value and adoption friction.
- Explain packaging decisions.
- Include upgrade and expansion paths.

## VC_Pitch.md

Write the investor narrative for VidyaAI.

Requirements:

- Explain the problem, wedge, moat, and market.
- Make the story credible for an education-tech investor.
- Keep the long-term scale visible.

## Fundraising.md

Describe the fundraising plan, use of funds, milestones, and investor profile.

Requirements:

- Show what milestones unlock the next round.
- Keep the ask grounded.
- Align fundraise timing with product evidence.
