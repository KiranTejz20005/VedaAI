# 01 Market Research Feature Prompts

Use these prompts when researching the market and validating the buyer.

## CompetitorAnalysis.md

Compare VidyaAI against generic AI assistants, LMS tools, ERP systems, assessment platforms, and accreditation workflow tools. Focus on capability gaps, workflow depth, buyer alignment, and institutional trust.

Requirements:

- Identify direct and indirect competitors.
- Explain where each competitor stops short.
- Highlight the difference between content generation and academic intelligence.

## TAM_SAM_SOM.md

Estimate the market in a way that matches the real buying motion of colleges and universities. Break the market into institution types, geography, program size, and adoption bands.

Requirements:

- Use transparent assumptions.
- Show the institution-level buyer model.
- Separate TAM, SAM, and SOM with clear reasoning.

## CustomerPersonas.md

Create detailed personas for principals, vice chancellors, deans, HODs, IQAC leads, academic coordinators, and faculty users. Focus on goals, pains, decision power, and what each persona needs to trust the system.

Requirements:

- Include both buyer and daily user personas.
- Distinguish between compliance pain and classroom pain.
- Make the personas operational, not generic.

## Pricing.md

Propose pricing tied to institutional value and deployment complexity. Show how pricing changes by institution size, feature set, data integration, and support model.

Requirements:

- Include at least one entry price and one enterprise tier.
- Explain what is included and what is metered.
- Connect pricing to ROI.

## MarketValidation.md

Describe how to validate demand with real institutions. Include interview goals, pilot signals, acceptance criteria, and what evidence would prove the product can sell.

Requirements:

- Focus on workflow pain, not feature liking.
- Define what counts as a qualified pilot.
- Include validation for the buyer and the end user.

## Alignment rule

Market research should always reinforce the institutional thesis and should never drift into consumer tutoring or generic chatbot territory.
