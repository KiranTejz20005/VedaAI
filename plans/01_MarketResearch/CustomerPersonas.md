# Customer personas
**Controller of Examinations:** accountable for process, confidentiality and timelines; needs governance and audit exports.  
**HOD/Dean:** accountable for outcome quality; needs coverage and programme visibility.  
**Faculty setter/reviewer:** needs fast drafting, editing and reliable references.  
**IT/Data Protection lead:** needs SSO, role controls, retention and vendor assurances.
**Principal/management:** owns institutional outcomes and budget; needs lower operational risk, readiness visibility and measurable return.
**IQAC/OBE/NBA/NAAC lead:** needs evidence completeness, attainment traceability and report-ready artefacts.

Interview each separately; do not treat the buyer as the sole user.
