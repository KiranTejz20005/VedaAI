# TAM / SAM / SOM
Use a bottoms-up model, not an unsupported revenue headline.

- **TAM:** Indian HEIs that conduct recurring internal or end-semester assessments × annual platform spend.
- **SAM:** target states, English-first programmes and digitally ready autonomous/private institutions.
- **SOM (36 months):** signed pilots converted to annual departmental or institutional contracts.

Maintain a spreadsheet with institution count, assessment volume, contract range, sales cycle, conversion and churn assumptions. AISHE is the denominator source; validate willingness to pay through signed pilot commitments.
